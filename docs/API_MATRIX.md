## Measured failures and attribution

Fresh persisted health-event groups include native adapter `InvalidStatus` 1,639, `ConnectionClosedError` 479, `SolanaRpcError` 36, timeout 8, DBAPIError 3, and PumpPortal connection closures 28, InvalidStatus 8, KeyError 123, budget-limit states 5, unfunded state 1. These are health-event counts, not one-to-one transport attempts. Legacy errors usually lack HTTP status/headers: **they do not establish 429 versus 403 or prove which account quota was exhausted**. The September operator report records an Alchemy monthly-capacity notification; current Alchemy health succeeds. Complete monthly attribution still needs account Usage export.

Local effective risk quotas are currently zero (unlimited), reassessment 10s, four candidate subscriptions and unlimited metered trade events. Two risk workers and a bounded queue protect memory, not billing. `.env.example` now supplies 180/hour, 2,000/day and 5,000 trade events/day; existing `.env` was not silently retuned. At six reads/pass, the daily cap models 360k monthly risk reads; entry/exit refresh and stream traffic must be added. Five continuously open curves refreshed every five seconds can add 2.592M reads/month; actual open positions at inspection were zero. Cache static authority metadata, keep mutable distribution/liquidity evidence fresh, and measure before selecting a paid tier.
